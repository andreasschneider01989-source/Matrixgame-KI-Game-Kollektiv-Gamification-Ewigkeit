# MACHX Second Earth — Quest 005: House reconstruction benchmark

**Date:** 2026-11-20  
**Status:** deterministic synthetic reference, single-/multi-view evidence tasks and build-plan baseline implemented; GitHub Actions PASS (41 tests total, including all 11 Quest 005 tests).

## User choices
- Object: house.
- Perspectives: both single-view and multi-view.
- Optimization: balanced; report geometry, material fidelity, inventory and estimated build time separately.

## Reference house
- Dataset ID: second-earth-house-001, version 1.0.0.
- Integer voxel grid; 1 m edge per voxel for this fixture.
- Bounding dimensions: 6 × 7 × 5 voxels.
- Full 6 × 5 stone foundation; perimeter wood walls at y=1..3 with front door and window openings; symmetric brick gable roof with eaves at y=4, slopes at y=5 and ridge at y=6.
- Reference geometry is generated by second-earth/reconstruction/house.py and described in house-001-reference.json.

This is explicitly SYNTHETIC_GROUND_TRUTH, not a measured real house, a photo reconstruction or an architectural safety design.

## Evidence conditions
- Single view: front projection only.
- Multi view: front, rear, left, right and top orthographic occupancy projections.
- Ground truth is withheld from the reconstruction role and reserved for evaluation.
- Orthographic projections are geometry proxies, not actual rendered photographs. The single-view task cannot uniquely recover hidden depth, back facade or interior. Those regions must remain UNKNOWN or be recorded as hypotheses.

## Agent roles
Perception, Geometry, Material, Architect, Resource, Builder, Physics, Critic/Evaluator and Teacher. The first six roles define the pipeline; actual learned perception, in-game construction and physics integration remain future work. The evaluator is independent of the builder.

## Balanced evaluation
- Geometry: voxel precision, recall, F1, IoU, dimensions and missing/extra blocks.
- Materials: accuracy on common occupied positions.
- Planning: inventory by material, stage coverage and build-time estimate from an explicit blocks/minute assumption.
- Keep all metrics visible; no opaque single score or arbitrary weights.

## Implemented
- house.py: deterministic house fixture, view task definitions, orthographic occupancy projections, material inventory and three-stage construction-plan estimate.
- test_house.py: 11 deterministic unit tests.
- house-001-reference.json: versioned reference metadata.
- QUEST_005_AGENTS.md: roles, evidence split, metrics and reproducibility rules.

## Research grounding
- HouseLayout3D (NeurIPS 2025) addresses complex real-world multi-floor layouts and structural elements including walls, floors, ceilings, doors, windows and stairs: https://houselayout3d.github.io/
- MineAnyBuild (NeurIPS 2025) provides about 4,000 spatial-planning tasks and evaluates open-world agents' spatial understanding, reasoning, creativity and spatial commonsense: https://mineanybuild.github.io/
- APT (2024) studies LLM-driven architectural planning and executable Minecraft blueprints: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.17255
- Multi-view reconstruction benchmarks emphasize calibrated views and independent 3D ground truth: https://martine-benchmark.github.io/

These works motivate our design; they do not validate this synthetic fixture or imply that a working photo-to-Minecraft model has been built.
