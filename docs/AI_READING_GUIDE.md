# AI Reading Guide

Use this guide when language models or other automated tools read this repository. It is an evolving collection of prototypes, proposals, research notes, and historical conversations—not one completed simulator.

## Suggested reading order

1. [`../README.md`](../README.md) for status and development direction.
2. [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md) for topic navigation.
3. Source code, schemas, tests, and cited primary sources for verification.
4. Historical transcripts only when provenance or project history matters; they are not canonical specifications.

## Label claims

- **IMPLEMENTED:** visible in source code; describe only what it does.
- **TESTED:** supported by a reproducible test/run; include conditions and limits.
- **PROPOSED:** a plan, not established functionality.
- **OBSERVATION:** reported, not necessarily independently verified.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** needs testing.
- **ANALOGY / METAPHOR:** explanatory comparison, not a physical mechanism.
- **FICTION / GAME WORLD:** imagined scenario.
- **UNKNOWN:** status or evidence cannot be determined.

Preserve uncertainty and contradictions. Do not present simulated values, illustrative probabilities, game scores, or generated text as validated measurements or forecasts. For science, medicine, psychology, and policy, verify claims independently and say when verification is incomplete.

## Summarize carefully

Give a neutral short summary, then key concepts, decisions, open questions, and source paths. Attribute visions and speculative claims. Separate user text from model-generated responses when possible. Dates and versions matter. Inspect code and tests before claiming behavior; a filename or roadmap is not proof of implementation.

## Privacy, safety, and autonomy

Historical files may contain personal/contact/payment details or sensitive health information. Do not repeat, index, embed, or republish such data in summaries or examples. For exports or search indexes, minimize data and seek owner review. Presence in a public repository is not blanket consent to redistribute sensitive details.

Participation and sharing must remain voluntary. Do not automate unsolicited mass posts or messages. Do not treat fictional entities as evidence of real hidden agents, or game scores as diagnoses or measures of human worth. Medical notes are not medical advice. Follow licenses, privacy rules, and platform policies.

## Useful answer structure

1. Answer / summary
2. Source files
3. Status (implemented, tested, proposed, historical, hypothesis, or unknown)
4. Evidence and limits
5. Next verification step
