# Human Flourishing 002 – Seven Domains, Will, and Responsible Measurement

**Status:** proposal / research design  
**Evidence class:** MODEL  
**Purpose:** turn the Matrixgame vision of “Paradise on Earth” into a humane, testable orientation without reducing people to scores.

## 1. Guiding idea

Human flourishing means that people and communities have the real opportunity to live healthy, meaningful, safe, free, connected and joyful lives, while preserving the ecological and social conditions that make flourishing possible for others and future generations.

“Paradise on Earth” is retained as an inspiring philosophical and narrative horizon, not as a guaranteed forecast or a scientifically established end state.

**Core principle:** improve the conditions for flourishing; never define a person's worth by a metric.

## 2. Seven connected domains

1. **Health** – physical and mental health, access to care, prevention, recovery and healthy environments.
2. **Freedom** – rights, autonomy, civil liberties, meaningful options and freedom from coercion.
3. **Will and meaning** – self-chosen goals, agency, motivation, purpose and the ability to act on considered intentions. Will is related to freedom but not identical to it: freedom concerns available options; agency concerns the ability to choose and pursue them.
4. **Relationships** – belonging, friendship, family and community ties, mutual trust, communication, respect and consent.
5. **Knowledge** – education, scientific literacy, critical thinking, creativity, open inquiry and access to reliable information.
6. **Safety** – personal security, peace, protection from violence, reliable infrastructure, material security and resilience.
7. **Joy** – positive experience, play, curiosity, culture, recreation, wonder and life satisfaction.

These are a proposed Matrixgame synthesis, not a validated psychometric scale. They overlap and influence one another. No domain should be treated as a substitute for another.

## 3. Evidence alignment

The OECD well-being framework provides an established multidimensional reference: 11 dimensions of current well-being, including health, knowledge and skills, environmental quality, subjective well-being, safety, social connections and civic engagement, plus resources for future well-being. The seven Matrixgame domains are a compact project-level synthesis, not an OECD instrument.

The WHO-5 is a short self-report measure of mental well-being over the previous two weeks. It can be a relevant optional reference for one part of the Health/Joy domains, but it does not measure the whole Human Flourishing model and is not a diagnosis.

Potential measurement layers:
- **Individual, voluntary:** self-chosen reflection, optional validated instruments, personal goals and qualitative notes.
- **Community:** access, participation, safety, trust, inclusion and distribution of outcomes.
- **Societal:** official indicators for health, education, income, safety, rights, environmental quality and social connection.
- **Planetary/future:** ecosystem integrity, resource use, climate resilience and intergenerational effects.

Never infer an individual's condition from population-level statistics or infer a society's flourishing from one person's report.

## 4. Measurement rules

- Keep a vector/profile of domains; do not collapse it into one universal “flourishing score”.
- Report distribution and inequalities, not only averages.
- Show missing data, uncertainty, time window, population and data provenance.
- Separate outcomes from inputs and proxies.
- Prefer reversible, low-risk interventions and evaluate unintended effects.
- Do not use sensitive health, sexuality, relationships, recovery or personal diary data in public repositories.
- No leaderboard, punishment, streak loss or XP penalty for low mood, illness, rest, uncertainty or changing goals.
- Any personal tracking must be voluntary, private, understandable and easy to stop.

## 5. Hawkins scale and spiritual interpretations

The Hawkins “Map of Consciousness”, enlightenment language, “fifth dimension” and collective ascension may be explored as **SPIRITUAL / PHILOSOPHICAL INTERPRETATIONS** or **FICTION / GAMEFICTION**. They are not established scientific measurements of consciousness, physical frequencies, or population-level progress.

Matrixgame policy:
- preserve the original idea and its meaning to the person;
- label its epistemic status clearly;
- do not convert its numerical levels into empirical facts;
- compare claims against independent evidence if a testable claim is proposed;
- never rank people by alleged consciousness level.

A spiritual concept can be meaningful to someone without being a validated scientific instrument. Respect and critical examination can coexist.

## 6. Conflict transformation and collective intelligence

Communication technology alone does not remove conflicts. Conflict also involves incompatible interests, power, fear, identity, scarcity, security dilemmas, misinformation and institutional incentives.

A collective-agent workflow should:
1. distinguish verified events from claims, interpretations and forecasts;
2. map affected groups and unequal exposure to harm;
3. identify interests and needs without assuming one side is homogeneous;
4. generate nonviolent options and test likely consequences;
5. preserve minority reports and uncertainty;
6. evaluate outcomes using safety, rights, fairness, trust and long-term resilience;
7. require human accountability for consequential decisions.

The system should optimize for harm reduction, agency, fair process and durable cooperation—not forced consensus or the appearance of harmony.

## 7. Proposed next experiments

**HF-002-A – Domain map:** review the seven domains with an explicit overlap map and identify blind spots.

**HF-002-B – Personal micro-action prototype:** private, optional, user-chosen action planning with no score and no sensitive data export. Do not implement persistent personal storage until a suitable private mechanism is selected.

**HF-002-C – Community conflict case study:** choose one documented, non-sensitive case; separate facts, actors, incentives, harms, interventions and measured outcomes. Do not claim causal impact without a suitable design.

**HF-002-D – Hawkins epistemic review:** catalogue the scale's claims, origins, measurement method and independent validation status. Keep it outside scientific model inputs unless a specific testable claim passes review.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

- All seven domains are represented and defined.
- Will/agency is distinguished from freedom without treating them as wholly separate.
- No universal aggregate score is produced.
- Hawkins and fifth-dimension concepts are explicitly labelled as spiritual/philosophical, not validated empirical measurements.
- Every proposed indicator has a source, unit, population, time window and limitation before use.
- Privacy, consent, accessibility, distributional fairness and the right to stop are design requirements.
- No forecast of a guaranteed utopia or inevitable collective ascension is presented as fact.
